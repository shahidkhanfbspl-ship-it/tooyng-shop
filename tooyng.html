01

The Canvas Philosophy

We do not print graphics onto mass-sourced blanks. Every piece starts with raw textile, sculpted and painted with instinctive brush-work that turns clothing into gallery-grade wearable art.

02

Bespoke Transformation

When you wear a garment crafted purely by human hands, something shifts internally. Our wearers do not blend into crowds—they carry an original creative statement.

03

Absolute Zero Waste

We do not maintain unsold warehouse heaps. By crafting strictly on a made-to-order model, every stitch and brushstroke exists solely because someone chose to commission it.

From the raw linen bolt to the painter's brush—no factories, no shortcuts, just focused hands.

01

Design & Form

Every silhouette is conceived with clean structural balance—neither oversized nor restrictive, engineered specifically to let the human form move naturally.

Cut & Stitch

04

Aesthetic Roots

Art & Philosophy

Original Continuous Line Form • Surat Atelier

"Instinctive strokes that reject overthinking. We express human emotion through line, not noise."

TOOYNG draws inspiration from early 20th-century cubist line-drawing, organic anatomy, and raw emotional resonance. Our drawings are never computer-generated or screen-printed in bulk.

The artist approaches each finished garment with a clear mind and a loaded brush. Some pieces carry bold front-facing motifs; others feature subtle storytelling whispers across the spine or cuff.

✦
Narrative Line Work

Faces that listen, contours that breathe, lines that celebrate human complexity.

✦
Emotion Over Perfection

Slight variations in pigment density and hand strokes ensure your garment is genuinely irreplaceable.

The Visionaries

The Hands Behind TOOYNG

Two brothers in Surat bringing together couture garment construction and fine art painting under one unapologetic label.

Fashion Artist & Creative Director

Tariq Ilyas

A fashion designer and fine artist who views clothing as kinetic sculpture. Tariq leads the creative direction, narrative line-art, and the direct hand-painting of each TOOYNG piece. His philosophy rejects mass trend-chasing in pursuit of genuine emotional resonance.

"We don't design for mannequins. We paint for the human spirit that seeks transformation."
Co-Founder & Operations Director

Ayaan Jamadar

The architectural backbone of the atelier. Ayaan oversees master construction, precision cutting, sample development, and experiential collaborations. He ensures that every TOOYNG garment satisfies the highest standards of structural longevity and fit.

"Art is only wearable when the cut and drape are impeccable. That balance is our obsession."
